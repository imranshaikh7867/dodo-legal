Delete your Dodo AI account

How to delete your account and what happens to your information

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Dodo AI and go to the Profile tab (the last icon at the bottom).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. This cannot be undone.

Can't use the app?

Write to us with the mobile number you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm with you and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Please use the contact email shown on the Dodo AI page in Google Play.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: mobile number, name, exam, language and profile picture.
  • All your chats with Dodo, the explanations and the stored voice clips.
  • Your practice tests, answers and scores, and your doubt history.
  • Your sign-in account and the notification token for your phone.

What is kept

Invoices for plans you bought are kept as long as tax law requires, with your name and mobile number removed. Copies in our service providers' backups are overwritten within a limited period.

See the privacy policy for more.
