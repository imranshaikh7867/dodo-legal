Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

About this policy

Dodo AI ("Dodo", "we", "us") is a study app for Class 11 and 12 students preparing for JEE and NEET. You ask a question out loud or by typing, and Dodo explains it step by step with working, drawings and a spoken explanation, then helps you practise.

This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Dodo AI app and to the services behind it.

Information you give us

  • Your mobile number, which you use to sign in. We send a one-time code to it by SMS.
  • Your name, the exam you are preparing for (JEE or NEET) and the language you want Dodo to use (English, Hindi or Hinglish).
  • A profile picture, only if you choose to upload one. Otherwise you get one of Dodo's built-in pictures.
  • The questions you ask Dodo, either typed or spoken, and your answers to check questions and practice tests.

Your voice

When you tap the microphone, Dodo records your question and sends the recording to a speech recognition service to turn it into text. We keep the text of your question. We do not keep the recording.

Dodo's spoken explanations are produced as audio from the text of each answer. We store these audio clips so you can listen to an explanation again without it being produced a second time.

Information created as you use Dodo

  • Your chats with Dodo: the questions you asked and the explanations Dodo gave, so you can reopen them later.
  • Your practice results: the topics you have learned, the tests you have taken, your answers and your scores.
  • Your doubts and plans: how many doubts you have used, which plans you bought, and your invoices. An invoice shows your name and mobile number. Purchases are made through Google Play (or the App Store on iPhone); we receive a reference for each purchase so we can confirm it, but never your card, UPI or bank details.
  • For study reminders: a notification token for your phone and your phone's time zone, so reminders arrive at a sensible local time.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the service, such as when you were last active and error logs.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not access your contacts, your location, or your photos, except a single photo you choose as your profile picture.
  • We do not use your questions or answers to train our own AI models.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To answer your questions in your chosen language and at the level of your exam, to show and speak the explanation, and to save it in your chats.
  • To build practice tests on the topics you have learned and to show your progress.
  • To count your doubts, deliver the plans you buy, and issue invoices.
  • To send study reminders, which you can switch off in Profile at any time.
  • To keep Dodo working and safe: preventing misuse, limiting abuse, and finding and fixing problems.
  • To meet our legal obligations, such as keeping tax records of purchases.

Services that process information for us

  • We use trusted service providers to run Dodo. They process information only to provide their service to us.
  • Google Firebase: phone number sign-in (including sending the SMS code) and delivering study reminders.
  • OpenAI: writing Dodo's explanations, drawings and practice questions. It receives the text of your question, the earlier questions in the same chat, your exam and your language. It does not receive your name or mobile number.
  • ElevenLabs: turning your spoken question into text, and producing Dodo's spoken explanations.
  • Cloudinary: storing profile pictures and the audio of Dodo's explanations.
  • MongoDB Atlas: our database, where your account, chats and results are kept.
  • Render: the servers that run Dodo.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store: taking payments for plans. Their own privacy policies apply to the payment.

Where your information is stored

Our service providers run their systems in data centres that may be outside India, including in the United States and Europe. They protect information using security measures and contractual safeguards.

Information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

  • We keep your account, chats and practice results while your account is active, so you can return to them.
  • You can delete any chat yourself in the Chats tab. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, chats, explanations, practice results, profile picture, stored voice clips, doubt history and sign-in account.
  • Invoices for plans you bought are kept as long as tax law requires, with your name and mobile number removed.
  • Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited period before they are overwritten. OpenAI may keep the requests it receives for up to 30 days to detect abuse, under its own policy.

Deleting your account

In the app: open Profile, scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm. This happens immediately and cannot be undone.

If you can no longer use the app, you can ask us to delete your account by writing to us from the contact details below with your registered mobile number. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Students under 18

  • Dodo is made for Class 11 and 12 students, and many of them are under 18. If you are under 18, please use Dodo with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian.
  • A parent or guardian can contact us at any time to see, correct or delete the information of a student in their care.
  • Dodo is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights and choices

  • See and correct your information: your name, exam, language and picture can be changed in Profile.
  • Delete your information: delete chats in the Chats tab, or delete your whole account in Profile.
  • Switch off reminders: in Profile, or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Ask us anything about your information, or raise a complaint, using the contact details below. We will reply as soon as we can, and within 30 days.

Keeping your information safe

We use encrypted connections, signed sign-in tokens, access-controlled databases and limits on how often services can be used. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Dodo changes. When we do, we will change the date at the top, and for important changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.

Contact us

Please use the contact email shown on the Dodo AI page in Google Play.
